# AG-Extension — One-Page Ask Sheet
> Companion to `docs/AG_PROJECT_CONCEPT_REVENUE_COCOA_COFFEE.md` (Sections 11–12). Sourced price cut: September 2026.

**Problem in two lines:** 1 officer per ~3,000 farmers; 25–30% yield lost and ~30% price lost to middlemen for lack of verifiable advice, grading, and traceability. Funders get paper logs instead of measurable return.

| | Grant — Pilot validation | Investment — Scale engine |
|---|---|---|
| **Funds** | Devices, EUDR baselines, satellite/AI feeds, enumerators + agronomists, SMS/IVR, M&E across 3 clusters (1,000–15,000 farmers) | Offline RAG, federated telemetry, EUDR/GS1 passport + FSP integrations, multi-region hardening, co-op onboarding |
| **Return** | Yield BCR vs. control, net premium/ton, verified coverage, women/youth reach, audit-ready evidence | Recurring SaaS + per-ha + per-batch + per-ton on passported contracted volume |
| **Condition** | Disburses against mapped parcels + signed buyer grade grids | Commercial close conditional on pilot BCR and buyer acceptance (Sec. 10) |
| **Amount** | Set by owner from Section 9 pipeline (co-ops, hectares, season dates) | Set by owner from unit-economics model (Section 7) |

**Use-of-funds shape (12-mo pilot):** ~60% field delivery · ~20% feeds + infra · ~15% engineering · ~5% MRV/safeguards. Grant covers field + feeds; investment covers engineering + scale. No sales hiring charged to grant.

**Milestones:** 0–3 mo mapped + baselined + grade grids signed → 3–6 mo NDVI/hazard/pest loops closed with evidence → 6–9 mo first passported lots sold on contract → 9–12 mo BCR published, one FSP live, carbon frame ready.

**Telemetry KPIs (same for both tranches):** verified visits · advisory resolution · yield delta/ha · net premium/ton vs. documented spot · passported + buyer-accepted share · payout/claim latency.

**Anchors:** ICE cocoa Sep-26 ~$6,049/t; COCOBOD GH¢41,392/t Light Crop 2026; ICO 272–287c/lb; carbon modeled $7–12/t (CCP upside only). No figure here is a guarantee — pilot price lists and baselines govern.

**Next step:** owner attaches Section 9 confirmations (3 co-ops, 1–2 buyers per chain, baselines, roster, entity) and prices the two amount boxes. Then circulate.
