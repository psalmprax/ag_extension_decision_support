## Slide 2: The Problem (Agronomy Knowledge & Labor Crisis)

### 🗣️ Verbatim Practice Script:
> *"To appreciate why this R&D is so essential, we must first look at the immense operational bottleneck facing global and rural Canadian agriculture today. In emerging and remote farming regions, there is a critical labor shortage: there is **only one human agricultural extension agent for every 3,000 farmers**. **[Pause]** This means over 85% of smallholder farmers operate in complete isolation without access to timely expert advice.*
> 
> *When a plant disease, frost risk, or pest infestation strikes, farmers often detect it weeks too late. As a result, preventable crop destruction destroys **25% to 30% of total annual harvest yields**, wiping out farmer livelihoods and threatening food supply chains.*
> 
> *Compounding this crisis is price opacity. Without access to real-time market data, farmers suffer a **30% price penalty** because they are forced to sell their harvest to local middlemen without knowing true regional commodity rates. This is not a problem that can be solved by hiring more human workers; it requires **scalable, automated software infrastructure**."*
